# PROJECT HAWK — ATTRIBUTIONS & CREDITS

HAWK is an independent native Apple application developed under the Project HAWK Master Engineering Directive. 

We gratefully acknowledge the following projects and individuals for their architectural insights, open protocols, and community services:

### Kushagra Singh (@kushagrasinghx)
- **Project:** BitChord (`https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord`)
- **Contribution:** Architectural inspiration for the multi-tier queue system (USER_QUEUE, CONTEXT, AUTOPLAY), YouTubei web remix client structure, and local monthly listening replay design.

### LRCLIB & Tran Chau
- **Project:** LRCLIB (`https://lrclib.net`)
- **Contribution:** Open-access, community-supported lyrics database delivering synchronized line-by-line musical lyrics.

### Signalsmith Audio
- **Project:** Signalsmith Stretch (`https://github.com/Signalsmith-Audio/signalsmith-stretch`)
- **Contribution:** Advanced audio time-stretching concepts and high-quality DSP analysis research.

### Binimum & AM-Lyrics Community
- **Contribution:** Inspiration for smooth Apple-style kinetic typography and word-synchronized lyrics animation.
